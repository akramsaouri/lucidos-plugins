Email Triage reads each of your mailboxes on a schedule and decides what actually needs you. Your own rules run first — top to bottom, first match wins — and anything they don't catch falls through to AI triage, which sorts it into needs you, FYI, or noise. Only the first bucket ever reaches you, as a single consolidated push.

  • One rule chain and one schedule per account, so a work inbox and a personal one can behave completely differently.
  • A notified-ledger per account, so a message that already pinged you never pings you twice — however many times it resurfaces.
  • Runs are silent by default. No new mail that needs you means no notification.

Setting this up needs a connected mailbox, a rule chain, and a schedule for each account — Lucidos can walk you through all three.
